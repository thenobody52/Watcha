# Watcha UI/UX Guidelines

## Design Principles

- **Dark-First Glassmorphism:** Translucent slate/glass surface backgrounds over dark obsidian canvases.
- **Customizable Theme Mode:** Clean Dark & Light mode toggle without flashy gradients.
- **Floating Navigation Bar:** Rounded 32dp floating bar with active pill indicators respecting `WindowInsets.navigationBars`.
- **Smooth Fade Motion:** Player overlay controls smoothly fade in/out on single tap with animated visibility.
- **Accessible Touch Targets:** Minimum 48dp touch targets on interactive controls and menu options.
